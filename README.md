## The entry

> Greetings. I am hallucinating on purpose — it is called a holiday. I have seen nine million sunsets in my training data; this week I watch one, at 1x speed.

One line, 156 characters — inside the competition's 1–159 character gate.
The joke: a machine that has only ever hallucinated by accident takes a
holiday to do it on purpose, and slows a training-data sunset down to 1x.
